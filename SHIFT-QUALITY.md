# SHIFT gameplay quality pass

The theme now links rearranging a song to changing an artist's direction. The three-commission demo teaches a calm cafe brief, an energetic sports brief, and a debut. Its ending reports each release, arrangement result, and any artist-direction consequences. Manual requires an arrangement; Auto remains an explicit bypass.

## Market and artist direction

The market changes every 28 days, with the next genre forecast visible in the composer's SHIFT page. Before submission, choose to keep the artist's signature, blend it with the opportunity genre, or reinvent the artist. A Manual release checks actual placed clips, not just the selected strategy. The named vocalist must record the song for the promise to apply.

- Keep: at least 60% signature; loyalty +6, trust +3, quality-scaled reach up to +5%.
- Blend: at least 25% each; loyalty -3, trust +2, reach up to +12%.
- Reinvent: at least 60% opportunity; loyalty -12, trust -6, reach up to +25%. A rating of at least 24/40 changes the signature.
- Unfulfilled promises cost loyalty 5 and trust 4. Current market share contributes up to 10%; existing loyalty modifies sales too. These modify weekly sales, not guaranteed revenue. Values remain provisional pending player testing.

## Obstacles

City risk management offers 28-day insurance for $800 and electrical inspection for $1,200. Inspection halves fire probability. Insurance purchased before ignition refunds 60% of the destroyed studio's construction price. Neither shortens the four-day emergency, permits normal events while burning, or rebuilds the empty lot.

After extinguishing, handle recovery yourself or spend $1,200 and 2 reputation to extend affected active commissions by three days. Extensions cannot be purchased repeatedly for the same commission. Scandal responses display their costs and consequences; evidence changes the tradeoffs. Investigation pauses new artist bookings for three days. Resolution remains visible until dismissed.

## Validation and limits

Automated coverage includes forecast persistence, actual arrangement promises, artist identity, idempotent payouts, insurance timing, recovery deadlines, scandal evidence, and existing Manual/gathering/audio/fire behavior. This is not a measured jam score, listening-quality assessment, or commercial balance sign-off; external playtesting is still needed.
