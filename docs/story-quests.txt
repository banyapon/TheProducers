Story Encounters — implementation notes

20 bilingual quests are defined in data/quests/story_quests.json.
Open Quest on the title page to browse every story. In a career, guests from assets/quests walk into the office on their own.
A guest appears when your Reputation reaches their gate (25, 45, 60, 75, then 90, four stories each) or by chance below that gate. Story prerequisites still apply. One guest at a time; they walk and idle near you. Talk to them to accept or send them away.
The career Quest menu lists only stories you have accepted. It does not list guests who have not arrived.
Commission quests create a real offer on the existing commissions board. Accept that offer, produce and release its song, then return to Quest to claim.
Collection quests count existing releases too. Remixes and tied primary genres do not count.
Rex requires HipHop 35/40 (score, not 35 songs); Vivian requires five Jazz songs.
Some quests require recorded Improvise notes, repeated hooks, energy rise, or a gentle arrangement; the exact thresholds are in each bilingual hint.
New briefs have a seven-game-day cooldown and cannot be issued while an earlier brief remains outstanding.
Passing a client, VIP, or legend story makes that person a regular client. Their later commissions are more likely to come back, and the reward multiplier still applies.
Passing an artist story unlocks them as a teammate you can romance. Signing still costs $4,000 for four weeks at $800/week, and the four-month workplace wait does not apply to them.
Completion, ending replay state, choices, song evidence, recruits and cooldowns persist inside Game.life, independently per save slot.
Rewards are marked claimed before emitting gameplay signals to prevent repeated claims.
Story contest trophies are deterministic story awards, separate from the normal contest cycle.

Art reference: assets/npc portrait-v2-2x2.png; assets/sprites/alice; npc-01-male-buzz; super-04.
New art: assets/quests/<id>/portrait-2x2.png, idle.png, walk.png and play.png.
Portrait cells are row-major: neutral, talking, blink, talking/blink.
Each animation is a separate 3x3 sheet with nine row-major frames, matching male, Alice and NPC sheets. Talk uses the idle body with portrait expressions.
Runtime uses nearest-neighbour sampling and virtual 160x160 frames anchored at (80,137), matching the original office cast.
All named characters are adults. Narin and Selene wear stage dresses; Kai wears a tank top and hip-hop cargo trousers.
Dr. Beep, Captain Orbit and Mr. Crumb are the three eccentric male characters.
Images generated with the built-in image generation tool from existing local references. Original source assets were not overwritten.
