# Arrangement and three-commission demo

The title's Demo button uses a separate `demo_career` save. It resumes that save on later visits. The three briefs are Morning Coffee, Final Sprint, and First Spotlight. Releasing all three produces a summary with a hall backdrop; victory in a contest is not required. The player may continue the studio afterwards. Declined or failed demo commissions are offered again. This is a scenario built on normal recording/release gameplay, not a measured 20-minute campaign; real-player pacing still needs testing.

Manual composition retains eight bars split into Intro (1–2), Verse (3–5), Hook (6–8). Section buttons toggle section looping. Palette selection auditions the clip; right-click deletes; Inspect mode opens copy/delete/rhythm/pitch/volume controls. Thirty undo snapshots are saved with the unfinished draft. Submitted arrangements discard edit history. The first demo brief limits the genre selector and pitch control; later briefs unlock them.

The suitability model is deterministic, not a judgement of musical beauty: brief tempo and density 40, energy contour and occupied-bar coverage 25, recurring melody signature in the hook 20, artist genre and pitch compatibility 15. Pitch compatibility is a deliberately simple heuristic, not harmonic analysis. Main genre defaults to the first Vocal's strongest skill; the demo supplies explicit artist directions. Live total scores are replaced by energy meters and density; Alice explains the first material mismatch. Submission gives the four-part breakdown. Idea bonus remains capped at 30 and feeds the existing production evaluation.

Validation: arrangement_test checks sparse versus dense cafe scoring, differing brief results, sports hook growth, undo/redo/copy, persisted drafts, resuming and the normal release_project path through all three demo stages. Existing idea/shift tests cover pointer drag, playback, tempo, pitch, MIDI, and existing saves. UI was inspected at 1600×900. No claim of commercial balance or final campaign duration is implied.
